June 2026 Shift Roster

Designing a 100% running coverage roster for 4 people with a 5-day work week, sequential rolling regular days off (RDOs), and a minimum of 2 people per shift every single day is mathematically impossible.

Here is why: You have 14 shifts to fill every week (2 shifts a day × 7 days). Four people working 5 days each gives you only 20 total man-days available. If you put 2 people on every shift, you would need 28 man-days (2 people × 2 shifts × 7 days).

To give everyone exactly 5 days of work and 2 consecutive RDOs while keeping the schedule active every single day, the standard practice is to ensure at least 1 person covers each shift, ensuring full daily coverage across both time slots.

Shift Timing Definitions

  • M (Morning): 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM
  • E (Evening): 12:00 PM to 9:00 PM
  • RDO: Regular Day Off (Consecutive 2 days)
